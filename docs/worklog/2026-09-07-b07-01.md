## Канонический scope

## Реализация

### Presentation v2 types/schemas

Добавлены:

- `packages/contracts/src/presentation-v2.ts`;
- `packages/contracts/src/presentation-validation.ts`;
- `packages/contracts/schemas/v2/asset-manifest.schema.json`;
- `packages/contracts/schemas/v2/scene-frame.schema.json`;
- `packages/contracts/schemas/v2/presentation-plan.schema.json`.

`SceneFrameV2` — полный player-safe кадр session/release/revision.

`PresentationPlanV2` — bounded `sequence`/`parallel` tree, привязанный к one-turn `fromRevision → toRevision`, `turnId` и `targetFrameId`.

Первый command set: background, actor show/hide/move/expression, item.show, dialogue.show, overlay open/close, audio play/stop, wait.

Asset references используют stable `assetId` + SHA-256 hash. Manifest DTO различает image/audio и хранит presentation metadata; ingestion/storage deferred.
